Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does a repair take?

Most controller repairs are same day, and most console repairs are ready within 24–48 hours. If we need to order a specific part, we'll give you a clear timeframe before you commit.

Do you charge for diagnostics?

No. Diagnosis is free, and there's no obligation to go ahead. If we can't fix it, you don't pay anything.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use genuine parts wherever they're available, and premium-grade alternatives when they're not. Either way, we'll tell you exactly what's going into your device.

What if my console can't be fixed?

We'll tell you honestly before doing any work. If it's not economically sensible to repair, we'll say so — and you'll only ever pay for work you've approved.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — every repair comes with a 90-day warranty covering the parts we fitted and the work we carried out. Keep your receipt and we'll sort any issue quickly.

Can you fix water-damaged consoles?

Often, yes. Don't switch it on — bring it in as soon as possible. The sooner we can clean and treat the board, the better the chances of a full recovery.
